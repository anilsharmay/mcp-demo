# United States of America Profile

### Overview
The United States of America, commonly known as the USA, is a country located primarily in North America. It is known for its diverse culture, rich history, and stunning geography. Comprised of 50 states, a federal district, and several territories, the USA stands as a beacon of democracy and freedom.

### Culture
American culture is largely influenced by various cultural groups, predominantly European settlers, but also includes significant contributions from Native Americans, African Americans, and many immigrant communities. The culture is characterized by a strong belief in individualism and freedom, which shapes various aspects of society including music, art, and cuisine.

Learn more about the culture of the USA: [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/United-States/Traditional-regions-of-the-United-States)  
[National Geographic Kids](https://www.natgeokids.com/uk/discover/geography/countries/usa-facts/)

### Geography
The USA is known for its varied landscapes which include mountains, plains, forests, and coastlines. Notable geographical features include the Rocky Mountains, the Great Plains, the Mississippi River, and the Grand Canyon. The country is bordered by Canada to the north, Mexico to the south, the Atlantic Ocean to the east, and the Pacific Ocean to the west.

Explore geographical facts about the USA: [Kids World Travel Guide](https://www.kids-world-travel-guide.com/usa-facts.html)  
[Tota World](https://www.tota.world/article/1290/)

### History
The history of the USA is marked by significant events including the American Revolution, the Civil War, and civil rights movements. Its formation as a nation began in 1776 with the Declaration of Independence, and it has grown into a leading global power with a strong influence in international affairs.

For more historical insights, visit: [Country Reports](https://www.countryreports.org/country/unitedstatesofamerica.htm)
